Contour Roam 2 settings

Storyteller is gone. Connect the camera or its memory card and change the settings here instead.

Put the camera's memory card in a card reader, or plug the camera itself into USB with the record slider back. Either way it mounts as a drive — pick that drive below. The settings file on it is usually hidden, so letting this page find it is easier than hunting for it yourself.

Drive not showing up?

This browser cannot save directly to the camera. You can still edit your settings here — load the file, make changes, and the page will hand you a new FW_RTC.txt to copy back onto the camera. For one-click saving, use Chrome, Edge, Brave or Arc.

Nothing leaves your computer — this page has no server. Source and issues on GitHub.
